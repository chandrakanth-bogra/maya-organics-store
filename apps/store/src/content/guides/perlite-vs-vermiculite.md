Both perlite and vermiculite are naturally occurring minerals that improve soil — but they do opposite things. Choosing the wrong one can work against your plants.

## The Core Difference

### Perlite

**Primary role: Drainage & Aeration.**

Perlite is porous but does not retain water. It opens up the soil to let water flow through freely and creates air pockets for roots to breathe.

### Vermiculite

**Primary role: Moisture Retention.**

Vermiculite acts like a sponge — absorbing water and nutrients and releasing them slowly to roots over time.

## Can I use both together?

Absolutely. A balanced "super soil" mix often uses equal parts coco peat, perlite, and vermiculite. You get drainage from the perlite and moisture retention from the vermiculite — the best of both worlds. This works particularly well for houseplants that need regular watering but are sensitive to root rot.

## Quick Reference

| Property | Perlite | Vermiculite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drainage | Excellent | Moderate |
| Moisture retention | Low | High |
